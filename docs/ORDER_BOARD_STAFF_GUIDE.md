# Swagramam Order Board: staff guide (1 page)

**Open:** `swagramam.in/board.html` on the counter phone or tablet. Enter the PIN (ask the owner). It stays logged in for 12 hours.
**Sound:** tap **Tap to turn on sound** once at the start of every shift. New orders then beep and the phone vibrates. Keep the screen on and the board open.

## The four lists
| List | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | A customer placed an order | Check the dishes. Tap **Accept**, or **Reject** with a reason |
| **Preparing** | Kitchen is cooking | When it is packed, tap **Ready for pickup** |
| **Ready** | Waiting for the customer | Tap **Send WhatsApp: order ready** (sends the customer a message). When they collect, tap **Collected** |
| **Done** | Collected, no-show, cancelled | Nothing |

## Money
- **Paid online (green):** already paid. Do NOT take money again.
- **Collect Rs X at the counter (yellow):** take the money, then tap **Collected and paid Rs X** and confirm the amount.
- The bar at the top shows today's totals: to collect, taken at the counter, paid online, tips, no-shows.

## Other buttons
- **Print:** prints an 80 mm kitchen ticket.
- **No-show:** the customer did not come. Two no-shows switch off pay at pickup for that number.
- **Reject** a paid online order: it goes to **Refund needed (owner)**. The owner refunds it in PayU. Tell the owner.
- A time in **red (LATE)** means the pickup time has passed and the order is not ready yet.
- Times are pickup times. Item notes are in red under the dish (for example "less spicy").

## If something is wrong
- "This order was already updated": someone else tapped it first. The list refreshes by itself.
- "Your session ended": enter the PIN again.
- Board not loading: check the internet, then call the owner. Customers can still order on WhatsApp.
